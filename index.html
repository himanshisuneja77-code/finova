What is Finova?

Finova is a modern finance tracking web application designed to help individuals and businesses manage their money efficiently. Track income, monitor expenses, and gain insights.

Whether you're a student or a professional, Finova simplifies financial management with an intuitive interface.

How to Use Finova 🚀

1. Signup / Login

Create your account or login to start tracking

2. Add Transactions

Record income & expenses easily

3. Track Analytics

Visual insights with bar & pie charts

4. Set Goals

Plan and achieve your savings goals
